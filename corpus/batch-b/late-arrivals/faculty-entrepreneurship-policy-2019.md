# Faculty Entrepreneurship and Technical Start-ups Policy

TEST FIXTURE: a fictional OLDER edition that arrives late, after a newer edition is already held (recency must fail and quarantine it). It is not a real university rule.

## 1. Purpose

This policy lets faculty members take part in a technical start-up that builds on their research while they remain employed by the university.

## 2. Equity share of the university

The university receives an equity share of five percent in a start-up that uses university facilities or intellectual property. The share is recorded in the start-up agreement that the research office signs before any facility is used.

## 3. Review of an application

The start-up committee reviews every application within sixty days of receiving it. The committee may ask the applicant for a business plan.

## 4. Time commitment

A faculty member who takes part in a start-up must keep a full teaching load and may spend at most two days a week on start-up work.
