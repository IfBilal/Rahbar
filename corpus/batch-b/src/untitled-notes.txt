the committee met on a rainy afternoon to talk about how the lab keys should be handed out to students who work late.
nobody could remember who had written the old sign on the lab door, so the group agreed to look for it in the storage room.
there was a long discussion about whether the keys should be signed for at the front desk or collected from the head of the department.
some members felt that a paper register was good enough while others preferred a simple spreadsheet kept by the lab assistant.
in the end the group decided to try both methods for a short while and then to compare how many keys went missing.
the lab assistant promised to bring a list of all the students who currently have access to the rooms.
a few people also asked whether the cleaning staff could be given a spare key and the group said that this needed a separate talk.
the meeting ended when the tea arrived and everybody went back to their own work.
