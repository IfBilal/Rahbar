# Faculty Entrepreneurship and Technical Start-ups Policy

TEST FIXTURE: a fictional amended edition written by the project group for Assignment 03 (a "newer version of an existing document"). It is not a real university rule.

## 1. Purpose

This policy lets faculty members take part in a technical start-up that builds on their research while they remain employed by the university.

## 2. Equity share of the university

The university receives an equity share of fifteen percent in a start-up that uses university facilities or intellectual property. The share is recorded in the start-up agreement that the research office signs before any facility is used.

## 3. Review of an application

The start-up committee reviews every application within forty five days of receiving it. The committee may ask the applicant for a business plan and for a statement about possible conflicts of interest.

## 4. Time commitment

A faculty member who takes part in a start-up must keep a full teaching load and may spend at most one day a week on start-up work, unless the dean approves a leave of absence in writing.

## 5. Conflict of interest

A faculty member must not supervise a student who works for the faculty member's own start-up, and must declare every such relationship to the head of the department.
