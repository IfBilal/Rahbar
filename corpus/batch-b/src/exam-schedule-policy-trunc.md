## 1. Publication of the schedule

The examination office publishes the final examination schedule at least three weeks before the first paper. The schedule lists the course code, the date, the start time and the room for every paper, and it is displayed on the student portal and on the notice boards of each department.

## 2. Clashes

A student who has two papers at the same time must apply to the examination office within three days of the publication of the schedule. The office then arranges a make-up slot on the same day or on the next working day.
