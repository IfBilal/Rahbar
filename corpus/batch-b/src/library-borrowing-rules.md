# Library Borrowing Rules

TEST FIXTURE: fictional content written by the project group for Assignment 03. It is not a real university rule.

## 1. Who may borrow

Every enrolled undergraduate student may borrow books from the central library on presentation of a valid student card. Faculty members and administrative staff may borrow on their staff card. Visitors may read in the library but may not take books out of the building.

## 2. Loan periods

Undergraduate students may keep a general collection book for fourteen days and may renew it twice, provided no other member has reserved it. Reference books and past examination papers never leave the library. Graduate students may keep a general collection book for twenty one days.

## 3. Fines

A book returned after its due date attracts a fine for every day it is late, as shown in the table below. A member who owes more than five hundred rupees in fines may not borrow again until the fines are paid at the accounts office.

| Item type | Fine per day (rupees) | Maximum fine (rupees) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| General book | 5 | 500 |
| Course reserve book | 20 | 1000 |
| Reference book | not lent | not applicable |

## 4. Lost books

A member who loses a book must report the loss to the circulation desk within seven days. The member then pays the current price of the book plus a handling charge of two hundred rupees, and the library gives a receipt for the payment.
