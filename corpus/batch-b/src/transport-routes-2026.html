Pick-up points

The university bus service connects the campus with five pick-up points in the city. Students who wish to use the service register at the transport office at the beginning of each semester and receive a bus card that must be shown to the driver.

Fees

The bus fee is eight thousand rupees for each semester. A student who stops using the service must return the bus card to the transport office to receive a refund of the unused months.
