# Scholarship Frequently Asked Questions

## 1. Who can apply for a need based scholarship?

Any enrolled undergraduate student whose family income is below the limit set by the financial aid office may apply. The application is submitted on the student portal before the end of the second week of the semester, together with an income certificate and the latest utility bills of the family.

## 2. How is the amount decided?

The financial aid committee decides the amount after studying the income certificate and the number of dependants in the family. A scholarship covers either half or the full amount of the tuition fee and never covers the transport or hostel charges.

## 3. How can a student keep the scholarship?

A scholarship holder must keep a cumulative grade point average of at least two point five and must not fail any course. A student who falls below this average in a semester is given one further semester to recover before the scholarship is withdrawn.

## 4. Merit scholarships

The top three students of every department by cumulative grade point average receive a merit scholarship equal to half of the tuition fee for the next year, and they do not need to apply for it.
